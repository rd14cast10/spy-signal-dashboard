How it's calculated▾

Cloud = BT Cloud color at the flip candle (EMA4 vs EMA20 and SMA20): agrees when Green on a Buy or Red on a Sell. BX = B-Xtrender short-term (Short 5/20/15) above 0 for Buy, below 0 for Sell; "BX flip" checks the flip candle, "BX c1" the flip candle or the next one. All 3 = Cloud agrees and BX c1. Result is scored same-day: from the open of the entry bar (candle 2 open, candle 3 if B-X confirms only on candle 2) to the close of the first of candle 18 after entry (entry bar = candle 1), the opposite flip, or the 3:30 PM bar (closes 3:45 PM ET). Trend = best move reached ≥0.25%; 0.15–0.25% = Choppy; otherwise Wrong Way if the close at exit was against the trade, else Choppy. Best = best move from entry in that window. Setup: ALL 3 badge, or n/3 = how many of the 3 conditions passed (Market Bias flip, Cloud agrees, BX c1). Gap = open vs prior close %: ● passes 0.25% (live), ○ below 0.25%. 54 signals.
